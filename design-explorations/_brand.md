## Adaptation
- Paper `#f5f5f7` (macOS window gray), ink `#1d1d1f`.
- Accent hue: system blue, darkened to `#0a6fe0` for contrast on light paper; secondary purple
  `#8e4ec6` (the accent used in the mock harness) for "pick" labels.
- Fonts: SF via `-apple-system`; display titles in `ui-serif` (New York) — Apple's own serif,
  echoing the quotation mark's editorial tone.
- Preview frames use real traffic-light colors.
